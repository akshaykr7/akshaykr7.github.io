Akshay Kumar

FARE Fellow and Ph.D. Scholar, Department of Mechanical Engineering, IIT Kanpur

I work on finite element analysis, vibration analysis and solid mechanics. My Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur was done through the Joint M.Tech–Ph.D. program, under the guidance of Prof. Nachiketa Tiwari. My thesis was submitted in July 2026, and the defence is expected in December 2026.

In my thesis I built a Nonlinear Waveguide Finite Element Method (NWFEM) framework to study the vibration of radial tyres, and I checked its results against ABAQUS simulations and against experiments. I am now a FARE Fellow at IIT Kanpur, where I extend this framework to include the air inside the tyre and the effect of tyre rotation. I also have experience with machine learning and computer vision.

Research interests

  • Finite element analysis
  • Vibration analysis
  • Solid mechanics
  • Machine learning
